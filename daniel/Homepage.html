About me

My name is Daniel an am web developer, what is web developer?


A web developer is a professional responsible for designing, coding, and maintaining websites and web applications. They utilize various programming languages such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and backend technologies like PHP, Python, or Ruby to build functional, user-friendly, and visually appealing online platforms. Web developers work closely with designers, content creators, and clients to ensure the website meets specific goals and provides a seamless user experience. Their role is crucial in today’s digital world, where a strong online presence can significantly impact a business’s success and reach.
